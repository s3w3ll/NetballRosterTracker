## Post Content

After being given a handwritten list of the team roation for a miniball team I was coaching and my wife (who had other commitments that week) was managing I thought I had it sorted. Until someone was injured during the game I had to rewrite the list on the fly - of course I didn't have a pen.

I then thought, there has to be a better way and with the Canterbury Netball Rainbow tournament coming up this year I thought I get my daughter's team more prepared - spreadsheets might not cut it while my numb fingers scroll through the cells.

So I built **netball.forgesync.co.nz**

The big thing for me was to manage games within a tournament with an auto-scheduler that distributes playing time and position variety across all matches automatically.

Next was the inevitable issue with injuries, so now I can update my plan and see how that will impact the times for that game and the rest of the tournament, and be able to identify who might need readjusting to ensure everyone gets fair experience in each position.

There are solutions out there, but for me I like to tinker away at things. 

If you're involved in netball clubs or grassroots sports administration and this sounds familiar, I'd like to hear how you actually manage it.
